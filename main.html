Cybersecuirty :

The term cybersecurity is a neologism constructed from the prefix cyber , of Greek origin, which reappeared in the middle of the 20th century with the word cybernetics , the latter concerning the study of control and communication processes in living beings and machines [ 2 ] . This prefix cyber has given rise, with the development of the Internet and the generalization of digital technology, to a large number of words such as cyberspace , cyberdefense , cyberattack , cybercrime , cybercafe , cyberculture , cyberdemocracy , cybermarket , cyber-reputation . It is in reaction to the risks associated with the omnipresence of information and communication technologies and their capacity for interconnection and data exchange that cybersecurity is gradually establishing itself as a new, full-fledged discipline (specialty) .

What is cybersecurity ? :

Cybersecurity involves protecting people, systems, and data from cyberattacks using various technologies, processes, and policies. At the enterprise level, cybersecurity is a key element of the overall risk management strategy , and more specifically, cyber risk management . Common cybersecurity threats include ransomware and other malware , phishing scams , data theft , and, more recently, attacks powered by artificial intelligence (AI). As cyber threats become more sophisticated and frequent, businesses are increasing their investments in prevention and mitigation. The International Data Corporation (IDC) forecasts that security spending will reach $377 billion by 2028.
